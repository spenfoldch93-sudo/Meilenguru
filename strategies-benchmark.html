How to read these benchmarks
  • Dynamic pricing means ranges, not fixed numbers. Miles & More and Flying Blue both price awards based on demand and timing. The ranges above reflect typical real-world redemptions, not theoretical minimums.
  • Always compare to cash price first. A redemption only makes sense if it beats what you'd pay in cash. Target redemptions above CHF 5 centimes per mile in Business, CHF 8+ in First.
  • The sweet spots still exist on partner airlines. M&M's fixed partner chart (Star Alliance airlines other than SWISS/LH/Austrian) often offers the best value — especially on routes to Asia, South America, and Africa.
  • The best redemptions are found, not planned. Being positioned with miles in your account and checking regularly — especially Flying Blue on the 1st of each month — is more important than any specific strategy document.
