Flying Blue Promo Awards
May 2026 —
the Swiss resident breakdown.

Every month on the 1st, Flying Blue drops discounted award pricing on selected routes. May 2026 has solid economy deals to North America from 18,750 miles. Business class discounts this month are largely locked behind the Flying Blue Extra subscription. Here is the full picture.

May 2026 at a glance — key facts
  • Book by: 31 May 2026
  • Travel window: 1 May – 31 October 2026
  • Discount: 25% off standard saver award pricing
  • Switzerland zone: Europe 1 — ZRH and GVA are eligible for European-origin discounts
  • Standard saver prices (one-way transatlantic): Economy 25,000 miles · Premium Economy 40,000 miles · Business 60,000 miles
  • Promo prices (one-way transatlantic): Economy 18,750 miles · Premium Economy 30,000 miles · Business 45,000 miles
  • Business class discounts: Only available this month for Flying Blue Extra subscribers

The economy deals —
genuinely good value

For standard Flying Blue members, May's headline deal is transatlantic economy from Europe at 18,750 miles one-way — a 25% reduction from the standard 25,000-mile floor. Given how easy Flying Blue miles are to accumulate (Amex MR transfers at 2:1 from Switzerland, Revolut (Premium and above) at 1:1), this is a meaningful discount for anyone planning a summer or autumn trip to North America.

The available routes cover most major US cities via either Air France (connecting through Paris CDG) or KLM (connecting through Amsterdam AMS). Both Paris and Amsterdam are quick, well-connected hops from Zürich or Geneva — add a positioning flight and you're on the same award ticket.

The Swiss angle

What this means
for Swiss residents

Switzerland sits in Flying Blue's Europe 1 zone — the same bracket as France, Germany, the UK, the Netherlands, and Belgium. This means all European-origin Promo Award discounts apply to flights departing from ZRH and GVA. You are not at any disadvantage compared to a French or Dutch member for these deals.

The practical play for May: if you have 37,500–56,250 Amex Membership Rewards points sitting in your Swiss Amex account, a round-trip economy award to New York is within reach at 37,500 miles return (18,750 × 2). At the 2:1 Swiss transfer ratio, that requires 75,000 MR points — a single Amex Platinum welcome bonus gets you there.

May is not the month for Business Class Promo Awards unless you are a Flying Blue Extra subscriber. It is, however, a solid month for economy redemptions to North America — and 18,750 miles one-way is a number worth acting on if the dates work.

How to book — step by step
  • 1. Go to flyingblue.com → Promo Awards
  • 2. Filter for European departures (Switzerland = Europe 1)
  • 3. Check availability on your target dates — leave dates blank to see the cheapest day across the month
  • 4. Confirm the Promo rate is showing before transferring any miles
  • 5. Transfer exactly the miles needed from Amex or Revolut
  • 6. Book immediately when miles arrive — do not wait overnight
