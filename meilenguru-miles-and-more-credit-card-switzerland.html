Miles & More Credit Card Switzerland —
Cornèrcard or Swisscard?

Two Swiss banks issue official Miles & More credit cards — Cornèrcard and Swisscard. Both credit the same mileage currency, but with different earn rates, fees and perks.

The
two issuers

A "Miles & More credit card" isn't issued by Lufthansa itself — it's issued by a partner bank that credits miles into the Miles & More programme. In Switzerland, that's Cornèrcard and Swisscard. Both credit the same mileage currency, but differ meaningfully in earn rate, annual fee and perks.

A note before you apply
  • Welcome bonuses, annual fees and earn rates change regularly — always check the issuer's site before applying
  • This guide is for information only — not financial advice

Option 2

Swisscard M&M
in detail

Swisscard is the official issuer of the SWISS Miles & More credit cards. If you fly SWISS regularly and value the brand integration, this is the natural fit — the earn rate is lower than Cornèrcard's, but it comes with SWISS-specific perks.

The limitations

  • Lower base earning rate than Cornèrcard

Swisscard M&M referral programme

Swisscard runs a member-gets-member referral programme: you get 10,000 bonus miles when you apply via a personal referral from a current cardholder. The referral code cannot be published publicly — ask someone you know who holds the card. Standard welcome bonus applies regardless.

The decision

Which card
for you

Quick decision
  • You want the most miles from everyday spend → Cornèrcard Gold. The 1 mile/CHF 1 Diners rate is the best base rate in Switzerland.
  • You fly SWISS regularly → Swisscard Platinum. Priority Pass and first-class check-in access matter for regular SWISS flyers.
  • You want both → Many Meilenguru readers hold both cards: Cornèrcard for everyday spending, Swisscard for the SWISS-specific perks.

Which bank issues the Miles & More credit card in Switzerland? +

Two banks: Cornèrcard and Swisscard. Both credit the same Miles & More mileage currency, but differ in earn rate, annual fee and perks.

Cornèrcard or Swisscard — which is better? +

For the highest earn rate from everyday spend: Cornèrcard (1 mile/CHF 1 on Diners). For SWISS-specific perks like first-class check-in and Priority Pass: Swisscard.

Can I hold both cards at once? +

Yes, there's no restriction on holding both cards in parallel — both credit miles into the same Miles & More account.
