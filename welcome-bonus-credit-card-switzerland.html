Welcome Bonus Credit Cards —
the 2026 overview

The welcome bonus in Switzerland is the fastest route to a large batch of miles — if you know where standard ends and promo begins. Here's how the four relevant Swiss credit card issuers compare.

What is
a welcome bonus?

A welcome bonus is a one-time miles or points credit you get when you receive a new credit card — independent of your ongoing spending. For most mile collectors, it's by far the most efficient route to a large batch of miles: a single card application often earns more than a full year of everyday spending.

A note before you apply
  • Welcome bonuses swing between a standard value and limited-time promotions — the figures below are reference points, always check the issuer's current site before applying
  • This guide is for information only — not financial advice
The comparison

Best welcome bonuses
in Switzerland

Four issuers are relevant for Swiss mile collectors: Cornèrcard, Swisscard, American Express (Swiss Platinum, usually applied for via Alpian), and Viseca (the only one that credits Flying Blue miles directly). They're independent of each other — you can apply for all four in parallel.

The standard figures above are the reliable floor. The real question is never "which card has the highest standard bonus" but "which issuer has a promotion running right now" — and that changes every few months.

The strategy

How to
maximise the bonus

Four levers
  • Timing: Promotional bonuses typically run several times a year — if you're not under time pressure, waiting for an active promo beats applying at the standard rate
  • Sequencing: Plan cards with a minimum-spend requirement first — clear the spending commitment before the next application, so both bonuses land cleanly
  • Stacking: Cornèrcard, Swisscard, Amex and Viseca are independent — in a good promo year, all four welcome bonuses are realistically combinable

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

What is a welcome bonus on a credit card? +

A one-time credit of miles or points when you receive a new card, usually with no or minimal spending requirement — the fastest way to earn a large batch of miles at once.

Are the advertised welcome bonuses always available? +

No. Issuers alternate between a standard bonus and limited-time promotions. Always check the issuer's current site before applying.

Can I earn multiple welcome bonuses at once? +

Yes — Cornèrcard, Swisscard, Amex and Viseca are independent issuers, so applying for multiple cards in parallel is possible.

Do I need to pay tax on a welcome bonus? +

Miles from welcome bonuses are currently not treated as taxable income in Switzerland. Consult a tax advisor for a binding assessment.
