Missing miles? —
here's how to get them credited.

You flew, you paid for the hotel, and the miles never showed up. It happens more often than you'd think — and it's usually a straightforward fix, as long as you act within the deadline.

What's covered

  1. The deadline — six months
  2. How long to wait first
  3. What you'll need to apply
  4. The process, step by step
  5. If the online claim doesn't work

The deadline —
six months

Miles & More allows retroactive credit for missing miles and points up to six months after the flight or partner activity. After that, a claim generally isn't possible — the clock starts on the date of the activity itself, not the date you notice something's missing.

In practice: note the date and don't let it slide. Five months in, you still have room. Seven months in, there's usually no way to claim it.

At a glance
  • Deadline: 6 months from the activity date
  • Cost: Filing a claim is free
  • Where: Online forms at miles-and-more.com, one per partner type
  • Requirement: A valid Miles & More account number entered at the time of the activity
Before you apply

How long to
wait first

Automatic crediting takes time — applying too early is one of the most common reasons a claim gets rejected, simply because the system hasn't processed the activity yet. Miles & More gives different windows depending on the partner type:

Activity Automatic credit usually lands within
Flights Up to 7 days after the flight
Hotel stays Up to 14 days after checkout
Online shopping / shopping partners Several weeks — until the merchant's return window has closed

Only once that window has passed and the miles still haven't appeared is a retroactive credit request worth filing.

Preparation

What you'll need
to apply

The documents you need depend on what you're claiming:

For flights
  • Miles & More account number
  • Ticket number (13 digits, on the ticket or in the booking confirmation)
  • Flight details: date, flight number, route
For partner activities (hotels, shopping, car rental)
  • Miles & More account number
  • Proof of purchase: invoice, receipt or booking confirmation — uploading it directly noticeably speeds up review

A common reason for rejection is simply that the name on the ticket doesn't exactly match the name on file in your Miles & More profile. Worth a quick check before you apply.

The claim

The process,
step by step

Four steps to retroactive credit
  • 1. Wait out the crediting window — see the table above, by activity type
  • 2. Open the online form — in your Miles & More account under "Retroactive credit", matching the relevant partner
  • 3. Submit your details and documents — account number, ticket or booking details, and proof of purchase if applicable
  • 4. Wait for processing — most online claims are reviewed automatically within a few days
If it stalls

If the online claim
doesn't work

If the miles still haven't been credited roughly 10-14 days after your online claim, the next step is Miles & More's general contact form — with your boarding pass or booking confirmation attached. Manual processing after that typically takes another 5-10 business days.

If it still doesn't resolve, contacting Miles & More customer service directly is the most reliable route to get to the bottom of it — particularly for edge cases like codeshare flights or travel-agency bookings, where the airline attribution isn't always clear-cut.

How long do I have to claim missing Miles & More miles? +

Six months from the date of the flight or partner activity. After that, retroactive credit generally isn't possible.

How long does automatic mileage crediting normally take? +

It depends on the partner type: up to 7 days for flights, up to 14 days after checkout for hotels, several weeks for shopping partners. It's only worth filing a claim after that window has passed.

What documents do I need for a retroactive credit request? +

For flights: account number, ticket number, and flight details. For other partners: account number plus proof of purchase (invoice, receipt, or booking confirmation).

What if my online claim doesn't get processed? +

After 10-14 days with no response, follow up via the contact form with your boarding pass or booking confirmation attached. Manual processing after that usually takes another 5-10 business days.
