The obvious starting point if you fly out of Zürich or Geneva — though SWISS long-haul departs Zürich only. Earn miles on SWISS, Lufthansa, Austrian and a bunch of partners you're probably already using. Air France · KLM · SkyTeam

Criminally underused by Swiss travellers. Every month, Flying Blue drops Promo Awards — discounted redemptions of up to 50% off. Most people don't know they exist. The ones who do book Business Class to New York for miles that others are spending on a flight to Berlin. Geneva-based flyers in particular tend to get more mileage out of this programme — GVA's Air France network is stronger than its SWISS long-haul options. It's also, in our own experience, simply the easier programme to find a Business Class seat with. The Viseca Flying Blue World Mastercard is the only Swiss card that credits this programme directly.

Membership Rewards · Switzerland

The engine behind both programmes. Swiss Amex cards earn Membership Rewards that transfer to both Miles & More and Flying Blue — making them the single most powerful earning tool for CHF-based redemptions. Timing your transfers is everything.
