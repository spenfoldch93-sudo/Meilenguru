Key M&M strategy notes
  • Dynamic pricing since June 2025: LH Group (SWISS, Lufthansa, Austrian) now uses dynamic award pricing. Star Alliance partners still use the fixed chart — often offering better value.
  • Mileage expiry: M&M award miles expire after 36 months without activity. A Cornèrcard or Swisscard with one eligible purchase per month protects your balance indefinitely.
  • First Class availability: Lufthansa First Class award space typically opens within 14 days of departure. Having miles ready in your account is essential — you can't accumulate in a rush.
  • Amex transfer: Swiss Amex Membership Rewards transfer to Miles & More at 2:1 (2 Amex points = 1 M&M mile). Factor this ratio into your strategy when holding Amex points.
