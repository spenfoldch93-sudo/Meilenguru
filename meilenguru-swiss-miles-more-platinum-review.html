Where this card fits —
the SWISS loyalist's card

Swisscard is the official issuer of the SWISS Miles & More credit card range. If you fly SWISS regularly and value the brand integration — premium check-in access, the SWISS logo on the card — this is the natural home product. The earning rate on the base Mastercard is lower than Cornèrcard's; what you're actually paying for is the soft benefits.

Time-sensitive as published: the elevated 40,000-mile offer plus three ZRH Comfort Vouchers referenced on this page was confirmed to run through 24 September 2026. If you're reading this after that date, verify the current figure at the Card Offers page or miles-and-more-cards.ch before applying.

We cover it alongside its competitors in our full Swiss credit card comparison — this page is the focused, standalone version.

The other side

The case for
the SWISS loyalist

Judged purely on miles per franc, this card loses to Cornèrcard on every metric — half the base earning rate, more than triple the fee, and a worse FX fee. That comparison is accurate and worth taking seriously before applying. It's also the wrong comparison if SWISS is genuinely your home carrier.

What Cornèrcard can't replicate
  • First Class check-in desk access at SWISS hubs, independent of your actual cabin
  • Brand integration that matters if SWISS status or recognition is part of your travel identity
  • 2.4 miles per CHF 2 on the Amex component — genuinely higher than Cornèrcard's Amex rate, if not its Diners rate

This card isn't competing with Cornèrcard on earning efficiency — it's competing on what happens at the check-in desk. For a genuine SWISS loyalist, that's a real trade worth making.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Is the SWISS Miles & More Platinum Duo worth it? +

Mainly for frequent SWISS flyers who value First Class check-in access and brand integration. The standard 10,000-mile welcome bonus and 2.5% FX fee trail Cornèrcard on pure earning economics — this card is bought for the soft benefits, not the base rate, outside of an active elevated promo.

What is the current elevated promo on this card? +

As published, 40,000 M&M miles (up from the standard 10,000) plus three ZRH Comfort Vouchers and a half-price first-year fee, running through 24 September 2026. Verify the current status on miles-and-more-cards.ch before applying, since this window closes on a fixed date.

How does this compare to Cornèrcard for SWISS flyers specifically? +

Cornèrcard earns more per franc spent and charges a lower fee, but doesn't offer SWISS First Class check-in desk access, the SWISS-branded card, or the Duo's higher Amex-component earning rate. For pure SWISS loyalists, the soft benefits can outweigh the earning-rate gap.
