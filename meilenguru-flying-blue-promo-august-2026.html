Flying Blue Promo Awards
August 2026 —
the Swiss resident breakdown.

Every month on the 1st, Flying Blue drops discounted award pricing on selected routes. August 2026 covers a far wider map than the usual North America roundups suggest — Economy and Premium Economy each come in two price tiers depending on distance, and three long-haul Business Class routes are open too. Here is the complete picture, pulled directly from Flying Blue's own Promo Rewards page.

August 2026 at a glance — key facts
  • Book by: 31 August 2026
  • Travel window: 1 August 2026 - 31 January 2027
  • Discount: up to 25% off standard saver award pricing
  • Switzerland zone: Europe 1 — ZRH and GVA are eligible for European-origin discounts
  • Economy promo prices: 18,750 miles (nearer zone) or 22,500 miles (farther zone), one-way
  • Premium Economy promo prices: 30,000 miles (nearer zone) or 37,500 miles (farther zone), one-way
  • Business Class promo price: 63,750 miles one-way — three routes only, all general public: Bogotá, Libreville, Cayenne. No North America Business Class discount this month.

The economy deals —
two tiers, more cities than expected

For standard Flying Blue members, August's headline economy price is 18,750 miles one-way, a 25% reduction from the standard 25,000-mile price, on three nearer-zone North American cities. A second, farther-zone tier at 22,500 miles one-way covers three Asian cities for every member, plus a wider Caribbean and Indian Ocean list for Flying Blue Extra subscribers. Both tiers are bookable for travel all the way through 31 January 2027.

The bigger story this month

The premium cabin picture

Business Class —
three routes, not the ones you'd expect

There is no North America Business Class Promo Award this month — if you were hoping for a discounted lie-flat seat to New York or Los Angeles, August doesn't have one. But Business Class discounts do exist this month, on three long-haul routes well off the usual transatlantic radar: Bogotá, Libreville, and Cayenne, all at 63,750 miles one-way.

All three routes are open to every Flying Blue member (no Extra subscription required), at 63,750 miles one-way — the standard 25% cut from the 85,000-mile Business Class saver rate for this longer-haul zone. None of these are typical leisure destinations for a Swiss resident, and 63,750 miles is a serious redemption, not a bargain — but if Bogotá, Libreville, or French Guiana are already on your list, or you're chasing a lie-flat long-haul sector purely for the experience, this is real value that most North America-focused deal roundups miss entirely.

The Swiss angle

What this means
for Swiss residents

Switzerland sits in Flying Blue's Europe 1 zone — the same bracket as France, Germany, the UK, the Netherlands, and Belgium. This means all European-origin Promo Award discounts apply to flights departing from ZRH and GVA. You are not at any disadvantage compared to a French or Dutch member for these deals.

The practical play for August: a round-trip economy award to one of the three North American cities is 37,500 miles return (18,750 × 2); to Seoul, Beijing, or Hong Kong it's 45,000 (22,500 × 2). A round-trip Premium Economy seat to any of the seven nearer-zone cities is 60,000 miles return (30,000 × 2) — the same miles as a one-way Business Class ticket at the standard transatlantic rate — while Fortaleza, Mumbai, or Shanghai run 75,000 return (37,500 × 2).

August isn't the month for a discounted lie-flat seat to North America. But Premium Economy from 30,000 miles one-way, across twelve cities spanning four continents, with a travel window running into 2027, might be the best value in this entire Promo Awards cycle — and if Bogotá, Libreville, or Cayenne are on your list, Business Class is quietly on the table too.
