Flying Blue —
the one everyone ignores

Criminally overlooked. Flying Blue has fewer earning options in Switzerland than Miles & More — but the monthly Promo Awards change everything. Discounts of 25–50% on award rates, every month, on a rotating set of routes. Most people don't know they exist. The ones who do book Business Class to New York for less than most people pay for Economy.

Earning Flying Blue miles from Switzerland

Transfer · Amex Switzerland

Amex Membership Rewards → Flying Blue

Swiss Amex Membership Rewards points transfer to Flying Blue at a 2:1 ratio (2 MR points = 1 Flying Blue mile). The 45,000-point Amex Platinum welcome bonus becomes 22,500 Flying Blue miles. While the ratio is less favourable than global Amex cards, the welcome bonus is still the fastest single source of Flying Blue miles available to Swiss residents.

Revolut → Flying Blue

Revolut RevPoints transfer to Flying Blue at 1:1. Available on Premium and above — Metal (CHF ~19/month) earns 1 RevPoint per CHF 2 spent, Ultra earns 1 per CHF 1. Accepted everywhere Mastercard is, filling the gaps where Amex isn't.

The Flying Blue Promo Awards advantage

Monthly promotion

What are Promo Awards?

Flying Blue releases discounted award rates on a rotating set of routes every month — typically 25–50% below standard dynamic pricing. Published on the first of each month. Routes change — consistency and watchfulness is rewarded.

Key Flying Blue strategy notes
  • Never transfer speculatively: Flying Blue uses dynamic pricing — confirm award availability on flyingblue.com first, then transfer. Prices can move significantly day to day.
  • Watch for Amex transfer bonuses: A 25% bonus has occurred multiple times. Waiting for a bonus before transferring can reduce the miles needed for the same flight by ~20%.
  • Promo Awards change monthly: Set a reminder for the 1st of each month. Routes from Geneva (GVA) and via Paris (CDG) or Amsterdam (AMS) regularly feature.
  • Miles expiry: Flying Blue miles expire after 24 months of inactivity. Any eligible activity (card spend, transfer, flight) resets the clock. Revolut transfers count as activity.
