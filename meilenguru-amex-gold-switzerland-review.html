Where this card fits —
the low-cost second Amex

Amex Gold isn't a smaller version of Amex Platinum with worse benefits across the board — it shares the same transfer ratios, the same programme flexibility, and a much lower entry fee. What it doesn't have is Priority Pass lounge access, and its standard welcome bonus is a third the size of Platinum's.

Promo status: the elevated 40,000-point offer referenced on this page ran through 25 August 2026 and has ended. The standard 15,000-point bonus applies now. Check the Card Offers page or americanexpress.ch directly for the current figure — a new promo may open at any time.

We cover it alongside its competitors in our Amex Membership Rewards transfer guide and the full Swiss credit card comparison — this page is the focused, standalone version.

The other side

The case for the
low-cost second Amex

Gold's standard bonus alone doesn't make a strong case — 15,000 MR points (7,500 miles after the 2:1 transfer) for a CHF 350 fee is unremarkable next to Cornèrcard's 10,000-mile bonus at CHF 220. Where Gold earns its place is specifically as a second card: applying for it after Platinum, or instead of Platinum when lounge access isn't a priority.

Gold isn't trying to be the best Amex. It's the cheapest way to hold a second Amex account and access its promo cycle again — that's a narrower job, but it's a real one.

The elevated promo changes this calculation substantially — 40,000 points at CHF 175 for the first year is genuinely competitive, closer to Amex Platinum's value proposition at a fraction of the fee. That's exactly why the current status matters more here than on almost any other card on this site: the standard and promotional versions of this card are barely comparable products.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Is the Swisscard Amex Gold worth it? +

At the standard 15,000-point welcome bonus, it's a marginal pick — a good low-cost second Amex for stacking, but not a standout on its own. During an elevated promo (verify the current status before applying — the last confirmed window ran through 25 August 2026), the calculation changes significantly.

Should I get Amex Gold or Amex Platinum? +

Platinum for the bigger one-time bonus and unlimited lounge access. Gold if you already have Platinum (or don't value lounge access) and want a second, cheaper Amex to stack bonuses across issuers — both share the same transfer ratios to Miles & More and Flying Blue.

Can I hold both Amex Platinum and Amex Gold at the same time? +

Yes — Swisscard doesn't restrict holding multiple Amex products simultaneously, and each carries its own separate welcome bonus. Stacking both is a legitimate way to maximise a single year's earning.
