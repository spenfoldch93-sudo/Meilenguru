Important: Swiss Amex MR transfer ratios differ from global rates. In Switzerland, Amex Membership Rewards transfer at 2:1 to virtually all airline partners (2 MR points = 1 mile) — including Miles & More, Flying Blue, Delta, Emirates, Etihad, Singapore Airlines, and ANA. British Airways Avios is slightly better at 3:2 (3 MR = 2 Avios). This is significantly worse than the 1:1 ratios available on US or some other Amex cards. Factor this into your strategy: the Amex Platinum welcome bonus of 45,000 MR points converts to 22,500 miles in most programmes. The earning rate on spend (1 MR/CHF 1) effectively yields 0.5 airline miles per CHF spent when transferred. This makes the Amex best used for its welcome bonus and as a flexible reserve — not as your primary day-to-day miles earner.
The golden rule of Amex points
  • Keep points flexible for as long as possible. Once transferred, they cannot be returned. Hold Amex points until you've confirmed award availability and are ready to book immediately.
  • Watch for transfer bonuses. Amex Switzerland periodically offers 25% or 30% bonuses on transfers to Flying Blue (and occasionally other partners). These represent a significant boost in value.
  • Points don't expire while the card is active. Unlike M&M or Flying Blue, Amex MR points remain valid as long as you hold an Amex card in Switzerland. This gives you time to wait for the right opportunity.
  • Combining programmes is the power move. Hold Amex points as a reserve. Build M&M separately through Cornèrcard. When the right award appears, deploy from whichever pool gives the best value.
