Lounge Access in Switzerland

Priority Pass comes bundled with several Swiss cards you might already be considering for the miles alone — but "included" means very different things depending on which one. Here's what each card actually gets you, and what it's worth buying standalone instead.

What is
Priority Pass?

Priority Pass is an independent airport lounge network — over 1,700 lounges worldwide, unconnected to any specific airline or alliance. It's not something Swiss card issuers built themselves; they buy membership access in bulk and bundle it into premium cards as a perk. That's why the terms vary so much between cards even though it's technically the same programme underneath.

The distinction that trips people up
  • Priority Pass lounges are third-party lounges open to anyone with a valid pass — at Zurich Airport, that's Marhaba, the two Aspire lounges, and Primeclass
  • SWISS's own lounges (First Class Lounge, Business Lounge, Aspire by SWISS) are separate and require SWISS elite status, a business/first class ticket, or a specific SWISS-branded benefit — a Priority Pass on its own doesn't get you into these

At Zurich Airport

Which lounges you get
at Zurich (ZRH)

A Priority Pass at ZRH gets you into four lounges, regardless of which card issued it:

  • Marhaba Lounge — landside/terminal, 05:15-20:00
  • Aspire Lounge, Terminal 1 — airside, Airside Centre, 06:00-21:00
  • Aspire Lounge, Terminal 4 — airside, 05:15-21:00
  • Primeclass Lounge, Terminal 6 — airside by Gate E46, accepts transit passengers (the Aspire lounges don't)

None of these are SWISS's own lounges. If SWISS First/Business Class or elite status is what you're actually after, that's a separate benefit entirely from Priority Pass — worth confirming before choosing a card on lounge access alone.

The alternative

Buying Priority Pass
standalone

If you don't want a new card at all, Priority Pass sells direct membership in three tiers:

Tier Annual price Visits included
Standard USD 99 None — USD 35 per visit
Standard Plus USD 329 10, then USD 35 each
Prestige USD 469 Unlimited

Prestige at USD 469/year is close to what Cornèrcard Platinum charges in CHF for unlimited access bundled with a card that also earns Miles & More miles on every purchase — the standalone route mainly makes sense if you specifically don't want another card in your wallet.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Which Swiss credit card includes Priority Pass? +

Amex Platinum and Cornèrcard Platinum both include unlimited Priority Pass Prestige. The SWISS Miles & More Platinum Duo includes it capped at 2 free visits/year (then USD 35 each). Cornèrcard Gold gives 5 free visits/year once CHF 7,500 is spent on the card. Amex Gold and Viseca don't include any lounge access.

How much does Priority Pass cost standalone? +

USD 99/year (Standard, pay per visit), USD 329/year (Standard Plus, 10 visits included), or USD 469/year (Prestige, unlimited) bought directly.

Which lounges can I access at Zurich Airport with Priority Pass? +

Marhaba Lounge, the two Aspire Lounges (Terminal 1 and Terminal 4), and the Primeclass Lounge (Terminal 6) — not SWISS's own First Class or Business lounges, which need SWISS elite status or a business/first class ticket instead.

Is a card with Priority Pass worth it if I don't fly often? +

Usually not on lounge access alone. The capped options (Swisscard's 2 visits, Cornèrcard Gold's spend-gated 5) suit occasional flyers; the unlimited cards only pay for themselves through lounge use if you fly often enough to actually use several visits a year.
