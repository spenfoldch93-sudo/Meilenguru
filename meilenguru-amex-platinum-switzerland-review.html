Where this card fits —
the flexible reserve

The Swisscard Amex Platinum isn't a Miles & More card at all — it earns Membership Rewards points, a flexible currency that transfers to Miles & More, Flying Blue, British Airways, Singapore Airlines, and several others. The reason it belongs on this site: the welcome bonus alone is worth more than a year of spending on almost any other Swiss card.

For the full breakdown of transfer ratios and when to move points, see the Amex Membership Rewards transfer guide. We cover the card alongside its competitors in our full Swiss credit card comparison too — this page is the focused, standalone version.

The other side

The case for
the flexible reserve

Judged purely on ongoing earning rate, Amex Platinum loses to Cornèrcard — the 2:1 Swiss transfer ratio halves the effective value of every franc spent once it lands in Miles & More or Flying Blue. That's a real, structural disadvantage, not a rounding error.

Why the bonus still matters
  • 45,000 MR points transferred at 2:1 is still 22,500 miles — more than double Cornèrcard Gold's standard welcome bonus, in a single event
  • The points aren't locked to one programme — a 22,500-mile-equivalent haul can go to whichever redemption is actually available when you need it
  • Priority Pass Prestige (unlimited) has real standalone value independent of the miles question

Amex Platinum isn't the card you keep for the earning rate — it's the card you apply for once, for the bonus, and keep for the flexibility. Judge it on those terms, not as a Cornèrcard competitor.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Is the Swisscard Amex Platinum worth it? +

For the welcome bonus alone, usually yes — 45,000 MR points standard is the single biggest one-time earning event available in Switzerland. The CHF 900 annual fee and 2:1 transfer ratio to Miles & More or Flying Blue make it less compelling as a long-term everyday card compared to Cornèrcard.

What can Amex Platinum Membership Rewards points be transferred to? +

Miles & More and Flying Blue at a 2:1 ratio in Switzerland, plus British Airways, Singapore Airlines, Etihad, and several other airline programmes. The Swiss transfer ratio is worse than US or UK Amex cards, which often transfer at 1:1.

Should I get Amex Platinum or Cornèrcard Gold first? +

If you're building a card setup from scratch, the welcome bonus size usually makes Amex Platinum worth applying for first — then Cornèrcard Gold for ongoing everyday spend, since its earning rate is more efficient than Amex's diluted 2:1 transfer.
